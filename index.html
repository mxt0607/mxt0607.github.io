Guijun Ma

Lecturer, School of Artificial Intelligence and Automation
Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)

I develop learning methods for intelligent systems that interact with the physical world. My research connects Physical AI, transfer learning, and industrial intelligence, with applications in robot learning, intelligent manufacturing, and lithium-ion battery prognostics.

My long-term goal is to enable robots and industrial systems to recognize hidden physical states, learn transferable dynamics, and make reliable decisions under limited data and changing environments.

Research Interests

Physical AI & Safe Generalization

Physics-informed learning, latent-state modeling, uncertainty, and reliable adaptation under distribution shift.

Robot Learning & Manipulation

Contact-rich manipulation, visual–tactile learning, mobile manipulation, and cross-task skill transfer.

Robotized Smart Manufacturing

Robotic machining, process prediction, calibration, and closed-loop optimization.

Battery Intelligence

State-of-health estimation, remaining-useful-life prediction, and personalized battery prognostics.

Background & Collaboration

Guijun Ma received his B.Eng. in Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation from HUST in 2017 and his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from HUST in 2023. From December 2021 to September 2022, he was a Visiting Scholar in the Department of Computer Science at Brunel University London.

At HUST, he collaborates within the intelligent manufacturing and automation community associated with Academician Han Ding and Professor Ye Yuan. He welcomes international collaboration on physical intelligence, transferable robot skills, visual–tactile manipulation, mobile manipulation in confined environments, and trustworthy industrial AI.
