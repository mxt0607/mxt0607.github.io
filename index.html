01 · Research

05 · Future vision

Physical Intelligence for Safe, Generalizable Robot Manipulation

01

Recognize hidden physical states

Infer contact, deformation, and task state from partial visual, force, and tactile observations.

02

Model action-conditioned evolution

Build compact, physics-aware world models that predict how interaction changes the scene.

03

Transfer skills safely

Adapt manipulation across objects, tasks, environments, and robot embodiments with limited new data.

04

Close the loop in the real world

Unify active perception, mobile positioning, and compliant action for dependable industrial autonomy.
